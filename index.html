Meet the Founder

Meera Nair

Meera grew up cooking between two kitchens — her grandmother's in Kerala and a family friend's taverna in Crete during summers spent on the coast. Recipe Secrets is the notebook she never stopped filling: scribbled ratios of soy to rice vinegar, and how many olives is "enough."

After years as a line cook and recipe developer, she started Recipe Secrets to make bold, authentic flavor achievable on a weeknight — every recipe here has been cooked, tweaked, and cooked again.
